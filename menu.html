UW CSE Schedule Builder

Welcome!

The UW CSE Schedule Builder is designed to help you choose classes for your quarter of choice! We understand how difficult it can be to narrow down and decide from the never-ending list of courses at UW as a CSE student, so we're here to help!

After selecting the quarter you’d like to plan for and the year you’re in, you have the option to pick courses from four different categories. These categories include: Computer Systems & Architecture, Algorithms & Theory, AI/Machine Learning, and Computer Applications. You'll then be asked to select the classes you have taken and passed from that category. Once you click 'Generate Course Option', you'll be presented with a lsit of classes you can choose for your schedule!
