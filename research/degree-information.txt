Completion Requirements
77-82 Credits

Science (5 credits): 5 credits from the list of approved natural science courses for Computer Science on the Allen School website.
Mathematics (15-19 credits) complete one of the following:
MATH 124, MATH 125, MATH 126, MATH 208
MATH 134, MATH 135, MATH 136

Fundamental Courses (24-25 credits): CSE 123 or CSE 143, CSE 311, CSE 312, CSE 331, CSE 332, CSE 351. Minimum 2.0 grade in each course.
Core and Electives: 33 additional 300-level and 400-level credits as follows:

Six courses from the CSE Core Courses list on the Allen School website of which at least four courses must be 400-level CSE courses.
Either one additional course from the CSE Core Courses list or one course from the CSE Capstone list on the Allen School website.
Additional courses from the CSE Electives list (which includes the CSE Core Courses list) on the Allen School website to bring the total for core and electives to 33 credits.
Minimum 2.00 cumulative GPA in all CSE courses.
